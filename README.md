## The Business Behind Luxury

An interactive business analytics dashboard exploring the financial performance, competitive scale, growth momentum, and brand portfolios of two leading global luxury groups: **LVMH** and **Richemont**.

---

## About the Project

This project combines my interests in **Business Analytics, Luxury Fashion, and Data Visualization**.

The dashboard explores:

- Revenue performance
- Competitive scale
- Growth momentum
- Luxury brand portfolios
- Strategic business insights

---

## Key Insights

### ✦ Scale Leadership
LVMH remains substantially larger than Richemont based on reported revenue.

### ✦ Momentum Shift
Richemont shows positive sales momentum across its latest reported fiscal years.

### ✦ Performance Pressure
LVMH's 2025 revenue remains below both its 2024 and 2023 levels.
